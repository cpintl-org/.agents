## What your decision means

- **Keep** = the proposed color becomes the official accent for that code in branded templates.
- **Change** = write in the note which color (only from the 7 approved palette) should be used instead.
- **Guide fixes** rows are not changeable; they track the guide's explicit assignment.

**To finalize:** send this sheet back with your ticks, or simply say "all approved" — the
helper records the outcome in `human-approval.yaml` and updates the plan kit.
